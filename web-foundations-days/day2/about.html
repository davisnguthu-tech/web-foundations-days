About QuickNotes

QuickNotes is a simple way to capture ideas the moment they come to you. Type a note, hit add, and it's saved to your list right away without the need for accounts. It has no clutter, just quick capture.

How to use QuickNotes

  1. Go to the Home page and find the "Add a note" section.
  2. Type your note into the input field.
  3. Click "Add Note" (or press Enter) to save it to your list.
  4. Review, revisit, or clear your notes any time from the "Your notes" section.

Features

  • Fast, distraction free note entry
  • Live note count so you always know how much you've captured
  • Clean, simple list view of all your notes
  • No sign up or account required

Keyboard shortcuts

Shortcut Action
Enter Add the current note
Ctrl + / (Cmd + / on Mac) Focus the note input field
Esc Clear the note input field
